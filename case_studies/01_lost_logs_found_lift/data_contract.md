# Data contract: Case 01

All timestamps are UTC. The experiment clock starts 2026-03-02T00:00:00Z (a Monday). Every table is written by `abf simulate case01`; the manifest at `data/manifests/case01.json` records seed, parameters, row counts and a content checksum per table.

| Table | Grain | Keys | Columns and types | Event definition | Source |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `products` | one row per eligible product | `product_id` (int64, unique) | `supply_line` (str: `ios`, `android`, `web`); `batch` (str: `8.1` to `8.6`, or `web`); `country` (str); `pre_period_finished_28d` (int32) | attributes as of first eligibility | simulated |
| `assignments` | one row per routed product | `product_id` | `arm` (str: `control`, `treatment`); `assigned_at` (datetime, tz-aware) | first arrival at the assembly stage, when the hash bucket is evaluated | simulated |
| `scans` | one row per scan | `event_id` (int64, unique); `product_id` | `scanned_at` (datetime); `station_version` (str: `v1` old station, `v2` new station) | the assembly station's scan as received by the scan log; none for products sent down the legacy manual route | simulated, with incident |
| `packing_runs` | one row per run of the packing step | `product_id` (non-unique) | `attempted_at` (datetime); `packing_step_error` (bool: the run raised a station error); `cycle_time_ms` (float, NaN when the station timing event is absent) | one row per run of the packing step, at least one per eligible product, through the station or, for products sent down the legacy manual route, the legacy route; a run is a visit to the packing step and is not a finished product, so most runs end without one; cycle time comes from the station's timing event | simulated |
| `finished_products` | one row per finished product | `finished_product_id` (int64, unique); `product_id` | `finished_at` (datetime); `received_at` (datetime); `amount` (float); `returned_at` (datetime, NaT when not returned) | finished product; `received_at` is when the records pipeline delivered it; `returned_at` is within 14 days of `finished_at` | simulated |
| `scan_log_health` | one row per `supply_line` × `batch` × day | `supply_line`, `batch`, `day` | `products` (int, products that reached the scanner); `event_loss_rate` (float) | 1 − logged scans / plant-side counts of products reaching the scanner, arms pooled | simulated monitoring table |
| `truth` | one row per product | `product_id` | `p_control`, `p_treatment` (float; equal for products sent down the legacy manual route); `y0`, `y1` (int8); `reached_scanner`, `scan_logged`, `in_older_batch` (bool) | potential outcomes, scanner-reach and incident flags | simulated; written for audit and read by no analysis (the operating-characteristic runner computes the same quantities in memory) |

Time semantics: `finished_at` is when the product was finished; `received_at` is when it became visible to analysis. The outcome window is 7 days from `assigned_at`. Data is complete at experiment end + 7 days + 36 hours (2026-03-24T12:00Z).

Joins (seed 20260928): `assignments` ⟕ `products` on `product_id` (280,000 rows, none unmatched); `assignments` ⟕ `scans` on `product_id` (233,889 products with a scan; 46,111 routed products without one: about 15% of each arm sent down the legacy manual route, plus about 4,200 new-station products from the older component batch (supply line A, batches 1 to 3) whose scan was lost); `assignments` ⟕ `packing_runs` on `product_id` (364,031 packing-step runs, every product at least one); `finished_products` ⟕ `assignments` on `product_id` (8,990 finished products, one per yielding product, none unmatched).

Known missingness: no scan for 15% of eligible products in both arms, which take the legacy manual route (`legacy_route_probability`; arm-independent and expected; these products cannot be affected by the station, so their two potential outcomes are equal). Plant-side counts exist only as the aggregates behind `scan_log_health`; there is no product-level plant-side log. Scans lost with probability 0.35 for products the new station handles from the older component batch (supply line A, batches 1 to 3), drawn at random within that batch (differential by arm; the incident; `incident.affects_arm` accepts `treatment`, `control` or both). Returns land up to 14 days after the finished product, so the return guardrail is incomplete for finished products completed in the 14 days before any analysis clock. Records delay uniform on 0 to 36 hours in both arms (non-differential). Cycle time missing exactly where the scan is missing, which includes every run served by the legacy route.

Data-generating constants (module-level in `assembly_incident.py`, not sourced from any real production line; change them and rerun): supply line shares 0.40 / 0.40 / 0.20 (B, A, C); batch shares 0.04 to 0.30 rising with batch number; yield logit offsets +0.30 supply line B, −0.30 supply line A, −0.30 additional for batches 1 to 3 (config key `older_batch_logit_offset`), +0.25 per log(1 + pre-period finished products); intercept calibrated so the old-station rate is exactly the configured 3.2%; pre-period finished products zero-inflated negative binomial (structural zeros 0.70 plus a negative binomial with mean 2.0 and dispersion 0.8, whose own zero probability is 0.37, so 81% of products outside the older component batch have no pre-period finished product; 0.78 and 1.6 for supply line A batches 1 to 3, giving 87%); packing-step error 4% per run; cycle time log-normal (median 1,200 ms, sigma 0.35); time from first eligibility to finished product exponential (mean 20 h, truncated to the 7-day window); amount log-normal (median 48, sigma 0.60); return probability 5% per finished product, uniform over 14 days, identical by arm. Routing is a hash of product id with a salt drawn from the seeded generator and recorded in the manifest (`realised.hash_salt`). Each block of the generator (covariates, arrival times, routing salt, station handling, outcomes, scan loss, finishing time) draws from its own random stream, and every per-product quantity is drawn by inverse CDF from a fixed number of uniforms per product, so a parameter change moves only what depends on it. Config values are validated on load, with an error naming the key.

Manifest: `data/manifests/case01.json`.
