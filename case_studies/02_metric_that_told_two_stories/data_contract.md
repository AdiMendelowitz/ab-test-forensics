# Data contract: Case 02

Every table is written by `abf simulate case02` under `data/generated/case02/`, one parquet file per table, and none is tracked; the manifest at `data/manifests/case02.json` records seed, parameters, row counts and a content checksum per table. Column names, dtypes, null counts, row counts and date ranges below are copied from the K2 inventory of 2026-10-12 (`notes/prepared/case02-schema-2026-10-12.md`, seven tables, 35 columns, zero nulls in every column, every row count equal to the manifest's `row_counts`); a column that inventory does not list is not described here.

Provenance. Decision of 30 September 2026 (decision record v2, section 8, "Case 02 data decision"): real control, simulated treatment. The base population is the EB-NeRD small variant (Ekstra Bladet News Recommendation Dataset, `train` split: 15,143 users, 232,887 logged page views, 18 to 25 May 2023). <!-- spec section 6 --> Active-user selection. EB-NeRD retained only active users, those with 5 to 1,000 clicks over 18 May to 8 June 2023 and at least 5 in-view articles (Kruse et al. 2024, section 2.3.1). <!-- README line 58 --> Raw data stays under `data/raw/`, untracked. Per-user and per-in-view frames are regenerated locally by `abf simulate case02` from the reader's own download and are never tracked. <!-- spec section 6 -->

Time semantics: `impression_time` is real; `received_at` adds the 0 to 36 hour pipeline delay identical across arms, as in v1.0. <!-- spec section 6 --> Three facts recorded from the K2 inventory as stated, cause not investigated here: `article_age_hours` is float with minimum -0.7394444346427917, so at least one in-view precedes its article's `published_time` under the spec's definition of age (`impression_time` minus `published_time`) <!-- K2 table 3; spec section 6 -->, and a count over the `train` split's 2,585,747 in-views finds 641 (0.025%) with a negative age, from -0.74 to -0.0003 hours, all below the first age tercile cut of 2.03 hours and therefore outside the old tier, while 19 in-views sit exactly on the old-tier cut, which is 33,249 seconds or 9.235833333333334 hours (9.2358 rounded), so the treatment of ties cannot move more than 19 in-views between tiers; the count and the tier assignment both compute age in float64 as `impression_time` minus `published_time`, in seconds divided by 3600, and the float32 `article_age_hours` column is a stored copy that does not decide the tier <!-- age count of 13 October 2026 over train/behaviors.parquet and articles.parquet; cut matches manifest base.tier_cut_hours 9.235833333333334; tier rule is age at or above the cut, with float64 age from `load_base` and `build_base` in src/abforensics/simulation/ranking_incident.py -->; `received_at` is `timestamp[ns]` while every other timestamp column is `timestamp[us]` <!-- K2 tables 2 to 6 -->; `received_at` runs to 2023-05-26 18:51:59.927817830 against a last `clicked_at` of 2023-05-25 06:59:58 <!-- K2 table 4 -->. Time zone: the inventory prints naive timestamps and records no zone. Window boundaries: `assigned_at` spans 2023-05-18 07:00:01 to 2023-05-25 06:53:37 and `impression_time` 2023-05-18 07:00:01 to 2023-05-25 06:59:58 <!-- K2 tables 2 and 3 -->; each user's observed 7-day rows form the control arm <!-- spec section 6 -->.

Joins: `draw_id` is the key of `users`, `impressions` and `feedback`, with `user_id` kept as an attribute <!-- spec section 6 -->; `impression_id` links `clicks` and `feedback` to `impressions`; `user_id` links `users` to `truth`, and both columns span 10068 to 2590637 <!-- K2 tables 1 and 7 -->. Expected row counts before and after each join were not measured by K2; the tables are long format so the notebook does real joins with logged row counts <!-- spec section 6 -->, and the manifest's `row_counts` gives the pre-join count of every table. Sample sizes above 15,143 per arm draw each user more than once (section 5 sets 57,600 per arm), so `users` holds 115,200 draws over 15,143 distinct population users, and `truth` holds one row per population user, not per draw <!-- spec section 6; K2 tables 1 and 7 -->.

Known missingness: no null in any of the 35 columns <!-- K2, all tables -->. Differential by arm by construction: in the treatment arm, suppression δ removes each of a user's low-quality in-views independently with probability δ, never the user's last in-view, and a removed in-view takes its click with it <!-- spec section 6 -->. Non-differential: the `received_at` delay of 0 to 36 hours is identical across arms <!-- spec section 6 -->. The population excludes users without a click: every logged user has at least one click in the 7 days (zero-click share 0.000), so E1 is clicks per reading user <!-- README line 58 -->.
